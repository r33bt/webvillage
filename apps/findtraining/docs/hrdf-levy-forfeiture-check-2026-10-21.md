## Verbatim from the circular PDF
- "the period of unutilised HRD levy will be revised to two (2) years from 1 January 2020."
- "The levy will become ineligible when the balance has not been utilised within a period of 24 months. This means no claims were made within two (2) years continuously."
- "a levy balance threshold of RM10,000 will remain for employers upon their ineligibility. Employers with less balance will not be affected."
- "The implementation date of the revised HRDF revised unutilised levy mechanism will effective on 1 January 2020."
- Legal basis quoted: Section 25, PSMB Act 2001.

## Superseded?
Read the whole circulars index (2010 to 2026, including Circulars 1/2026 to 3/2026). No later circular is about the unutilised-levy period or forfeiture. Later items touching levy balances are different matters: 5/2024 and 2/2024 (Program Latihan MADANI, using 15 per cent of the remaining unused levy balance), 1/2026 (levy payment exemption for Education industries), 2/2025 (payment deadline deferment).

## Limits (why this is not stronger)
- "No superseding circular" rests on the circulars index only. Terms can also change through guidelines or application rules that are not circulars; the support-centre article could not be read as text.
- The circular PDF gives no issue date, only the 1 January 2020 effective date.
- The worked examples in the circular are for the 2018 to 2019 non-utilisation window and are illustrative only.

## Decision
Not added to any public page. The brief allows it only with a dated primary source and verified currency; the missing issue date and the index-only absence check fall short of that. Re-check the support-centre article in a browser before using it. No existing page needed the line to be useful.
